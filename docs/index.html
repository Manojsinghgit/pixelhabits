What we collect

PixelHabits collects only what's needed to run the habit tracker you're using:

Account information

Username, password (stored as a salted hash — we never see or store it in plain text), and an optional email address if you provide one.

Habit data you create

Habit names, categories, icons, colors, completion logs, streaks, and any notes you write for yourself. This is the core content of the app.

Reminder settings

If you set a reminder time for a habit, that time is stored so the app can notify you. On Android, if you enable reminders, a device push token is registered so a notification can reach you even when the app is closed.

Step count

Read directly from your device's motion sensor to show today's step count on-screen. This stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

What we don't collect

No hidden extras. Specifically, this app does not use:

✕ Advertising SDKs ✕ Analytics/tracking ✕ Third-party trackers ✕ Location data ✕ Contacts access ✕ Ad identifiers

How it's used

  • To create and secure your account, and let you log in.
  • To store and display your habits, streaks, and history back to you.
  • To compute account-wide stats: XP, levels, achievements, and weekly insights.
  • To deliver the reminders you set, and (on Android, if enabled) push notifications when the app is closed.
  • If you add friends in the app, to show your username, level, streak, and weekly completion percentage to the friends you've connected with — and nothing else.

Storage & security

Your data is stored in a private database on our hosting provider (Render), accessible only through the app's own login. Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

Sharing

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. It isn't shared with third parties at all, except:

  • Friends you explicitly add — see limited stats listed above, and only after you both accept the connection.
  • Our hosting providers (Render for the backend, Expo/Google for push notification delivery) — who process data solely to run the service, not for their own purposes.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us (below). We'll confirm and permanently delete your account, habits, logs, and notes.

Children

PixelHabits is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. We won't change how existing data is used without telling you.

Contact

Questions, or want your data deleted? Reach out at manoj@payairo.com.
